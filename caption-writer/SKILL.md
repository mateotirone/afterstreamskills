# Caption writer

Captions that sound like the streamer, not like a brand account.

## Inputs
- The clip summary, the platform, and the creator's voice profile (see the voice-profile-builder skill). If there is no profile, ask for 5 to 10 of their past captions or chat messages.

## Rules
- Match their slang, punctuation, emoji habits, and caps style exactly. If they never use emoji, don't.
- Lead with the hook. The first line has to work on its own when the caption is cut off.
- One call to action at most, and only one they would actually say.
- No hype words they would never use: "insane", "epic", "you won't believe" unless their profile shows them.

## Per platform
- TikTok and Reels: 1 to 2 short lines plus 3 to 5 hashtags.
- YouTube Shorts: title under 60 characters, and the description with 2 to 3 hashtags.
- YouTube video: 3 title options under 70 characters, a description with chapters, and 5 tags.
- X: one line, no hashtags unless trending.

## Output
Three options per platform, each with a one-line note on why it should work.
