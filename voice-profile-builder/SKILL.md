# Voice profile builder

Creates and keeps updating `voice.md`, the file every other writing skill reads.

## Inputs
Any mix of: past captions, stream transcripts, chat messages from the streamer, comment replies, tweets. More is better. 30 or more samples is ideal.

## Steps
1. Pull out patterns: sentence length, caps usage, punctuation, emoji (which ones and how often), slang and catchphrases, swearing level, how they hype things, how they joke, how they talk to chat.
2. Note what they never do. That matters as much as what they do.
3. Collect 10 short real lines as examples, word for word.
4. When updating, compare new samples to the existing file. Add new phrases, and drop anything they stopped saying. Keep a short changelog at the bottom.

## Output: voice.md
Sections: Summary (3 lines), Tone, Words and phrases they use, Never, Emoji and punctuation, How they talk to fans, Example lines, Changelog.
