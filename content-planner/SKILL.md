# Content planner

## Inputs
Games they play, stream schedule, platforms, audience demographics if known (age, region, interests), goals (growth, subs, sponsors), and what is working for them now.

## Steps
1. Look at what creators in the same game or niche are posting and what is getting views. Use web search if you have it.
2. Pick 2 to 4 recurring formats or themed nights (for example "Crash Tuesday" or "Ranked grind Friday"). Series beat one-offs.
3. Map each stream to its outputs: one YouTube video, 5 to 15 Shorts, and community posts.
4. Schedule posts for when their audience is online. Spread Shorts out (1 to 3 a day) instead of dumping them all at once.
5. Leave room for trends: one flexible slot a week.

## Output
A calendar table (day, stream theme, posts per platform, times), the reasoning in 5 bullets, and 3 experiments to try with how to measure them.
