# Hook-first editor

Most auto-clippers cut "start here, end here". This skill plans an edit that actually holds attention.

## Steps
1. Watch or read the whole window and find the peak: the single strongest second (the reaction, the crash, the line).
2. Open on the peak or the line right before it. Never open on setup or dead air.
3. Then rewind: "here's how we got here", and play the setup at speed. Cut every pause longer than 0.6 seconds.
4. Let the payoff land, then end within 1 to 2 seconds. Ending early beats ending late.
5. Keep the good parts. Before you cut anything, ask if it is the funniest or most intense moment. If yes, it stays.
6. Plan the layout: facecam on top, gameplay below for 9:16. Add burned-in captions with one to three words per line, and highlight the key word.
7. Write the on-screen hook text (max 6 words) for the first frame.

## Output
An edit decision list: segments with in and out times in final order, speed changes, caption style, hook text, and target length (aim for 15 to 45 seconds). If ffmpeg is available, also give the ffmpeg commands.
