# YouTube packager

## Inputs
The highlight list or video outline, the creator's voice profile, and the game.

## Steps
1. Find the one story of the video. A good highlight video is about a single thing ("I finally won at Spa", "the worst lobby ever").
2. Write 5 titles under 70 characters. Mix curiosity, stakes, and plain clarity. Avoid all caps and clickbait that the video can't pay off.
3. Write 3 thumbnail concepts: the main subject, the facial expression, up to 4 words of text, and colors that pop against the game's palette. Thumbnail text should add to the title, not repeat it.
4. Write chapters from the timestamps, with the first chapter at 0:00.
5. Write the description: 2 hook lines, chapters, links, and 3 hashtags.

## Output
Titles ranked, thumbnail concepts (detailed enough to hand to an image model), chapters, and the description.
