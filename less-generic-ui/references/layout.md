# Layout rules

## Heights and screens
- Plan at 1440x900 and 390x844. Take a screenshot at every full screen while scrolling, and each one should show one section.
- Give each section 0.5 to 1.25 screens of content and about a quarter screen of space between sections.
- If a section's content is under 360px tall at 1440, merge it into its neighbor or give it a picture.
- If it's between 1 and 1.5 screens tall, cut it to one screen or split it into two sections, each with its own heading.

## Edges
- Every section uses the same content width and the same left and right edges. One wrapper class does this for all of them.
- Use the same gutter on both sides. On phones that's 16 to 20px.

## Heroes that don't look like a template
Choose one of these. Don't default to centered text over a gradient.
- **Split:** copy on the left at about 5 of 12 columns, the product UI on the right at 7.
- **Product:** the product large and whole, with the h1 above or beside it.
- **Statement:** a very large h1 alone, with the proof in the next section.
- **Photo:** one full-bleed real photo, with the h1 on a calm part of it.

## Sections that work instead of three cards
- Copy beside a single diagram card (input, then the process, then 2 to 4 outputs, joined with lines).
- A real UI fragment, such as a calendar, a review screen or a timeline, with two or three short rules beside it.
- A zig-zag: alternating copy and picture, each pair different in size.
- A bento grid with one large tile, only when the tiles really differ in importance.

## Closing
End with one screen: a big statement, one button and a single line of reassurance. Put the footer links below it, small.

## Phone
- Use one column. The picture can go above or below the copy, so pick whichever reads better.
- Hide decorative lines and extra columns rather than squeezing them.
- Make tap targets at least 44px. Nothing should scroll sideways.
