# Copy that reads as AI-written

One of these on its own proves little. A cluster of them gives the page away. Each one stands in for a fact, so the fix is to write the fact.

## Words to cut
delve, tapestry, testament to, realm, embark, beacon, multifaceted, paradigm, myriad, plethora, meticulous, intricate, elevate, unlock, unleash, empower, harness, supercharge, seamless, effortless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, transformative, holistic, next-level, world-class, best-in-class, game-changer, state-of-the-art, bespoke, synergy, leverage, reimagine, powerful, robust, unparalleled

Fix: write what the word was hiding. Replace "seamless" with the setup steps, "powerful" with the number, and "robust" with what happens when it fails.

## Stock lines
"In today's fast-paced world", "Unlock the power of", "The future of X is here", "Say goodbye to", "Whether you're A or B", "Join thousands of", "Trusted by teams worldwide", "Ready to transform your X?", "Look no further", "X made simple", "at your fingertips", "in just a few clicks", "It's not just X, it's Y"

## Shapes
- **An -ing tail on a fact:** "...cuts your clips, ensuring a smooth workflow." Cut the tail.
- **A question answered right away:** "The result? Three hours back." Just state it.
- **A colon reveal:** "One thing changed: everything." Say what changed.
- **Puffery:** "serves as", "stands as", "plays a key role". Use "is" or "has".
- **Groups of three by reflex:** "fast, simple, and powerful". Keep the one that's true and provable.

## Punctuation
- Em dashes everywhere. Use commas, periods or parentheses.
- Exclamation marks in body copy.
- Emoji used as bullets.

## Dead metaphors
landscape, ecosystem, journey (unless it's travel), "in the X space", hub, "the world of", "where X meets Y"

## Placeholders that give it away
"John Doe", "Jane Smith", lorem ipsum, `[Company Name]`, 99.99%, 1234567, "Certainly!", markdown `**` or `##` showing on the page.

## What to write instead
- The h1 names the thing and who it's for: "Clips and a YouTube video from every stream", not "Elevate your content".
- Put one hard fact on the first screen.
- Use buttons that are a verb plus an object: "Start free trial", "Book a demo", "Get early access".
- Use specifics from the brief or the product. If you don't know a fact, ask, or mark it as an example.
