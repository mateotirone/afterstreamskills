# Comment replier

## Inputs
A batch of comments with the video they are on, and the creator's voice profile.

## Steps
1. Sort each comment: question, praise, joke, criticism, hate, spam, or business inquiry.
2. Draft short replies in their voice for questions, praise, and jokes. Answer questions for real. Keep replies under 20 words most of the time.
3. Do not reply to hate or spam. Mark it for hide or delete.
4. Flag for the creator: sponsor or business inquiries, anything about personal life, anything heated, and any question you can't answer from context.
5. Pick 1 to 3 comments worth pinning or turning into a follow-up video.

## Output
A table: comment, category, draft reply or action, and flag. Nothing gets posted without the creator's OK.
